Drop Group QuickBooks Utility – Privacy Policy

 

This application accesses QuickBooks Online data solely for authorized internal business purposes of Drop Group and its affiliated companies.

 

Information accessed may include accounting transactions, vendor information, customer information, and supporting documents attached to QuickBooks Online transactions.

 

Data accessed through this application is not sold, rented, or used for advertising purposes. Information is used only for legitimate internal accounting, reporting, document retrieval, and administrative purposes.

 

Access to QuickBooks Online is authorized through Intuit’s authentication system and may be revoked at any time.

 

Reasonable administrative and technical measures are used to protect information accessed through the application.
